Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the biggest domestic film ever

Tom Holland’s fourth solo outing just overtook Star Wars: The Force Awakens ($936.7M) as North America’s all-time box-office champ after 47 days — about $2.45B worldwide (No. 3 all-time). It’s the exclamation point on a record 2026 summer and still the movie everyone’s measuring everything else against.
