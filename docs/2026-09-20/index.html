Sunday, September 20, 2026 · What’s actually dominating today
Four stories owning the conversation today: Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil smashes the franchise open, Ed Sheeran turns a brutal week into a Philly catharsis, New Found Glory’s Chad Gilbert is mourned at 45, and Jhené Aiko finally lands her first Billboard 200 No. 1.
Movies

Resident Evil opens to a franchise-record $60 million

Zach Cregger’s reboot didn’t just clear the bar — it reset it. Sony estimates a $60M domestic opening (vs. a $40–50M track) and $108.3M worldwide, both franchise highs, with a Certified Fresh ~96% on Rotten Tomatoes. Austin Abrams stars as a medical courier sprinting through Raccoon City; word of mouth is carrying what last weekend’s soft Practical Magic 2 couldn’t.

Live Music

Ed Sheeran almost canceled the tour — then Philly saved the night

At Lincoln Financial Field on Saturday, Sheeran called the past week one of the worst of his life after Macklemore’s removal and a cascade of openers quitting. He said he considered scrapping the LOOP tour entirely — until ticket sales showed 50,000 fans still showing up. Closing speech: “Tonight has been honestly the best show of my life.”

Music

New Found Glory’s Chad Gilbert dies at 45

The founding guitarist and co-songwriter behind early-2000s pop-punk staples like “My Friends Over You” died peacefully in his sleep this morning after a years-long fight with adrenal cancer, including emergency brain surgery earlier this year. His wife Lisa and bandmates remembered him as a husband and father first — and vowed his spirit will be on every stage they play.

Charts

Jhené Aiko scores her first Billboard 200 No. 1 with Westside Whimsy

Six years after Chilombo, Aiko’s fourth studio album bows at No. 1 with 74,000 equivalent units — the first R&B album by a woman to top the chart this year, and the first since SZA’s SOS. Features from Kendrick Lamar, Tyga, and Larry June; it was also the only new top-10 debut this week.
