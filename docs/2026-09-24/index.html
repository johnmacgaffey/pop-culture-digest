Four stories owning the conversation this morning: Taylor Swift’s Showgirl Encore lands tomorrow with a “Patient Zero” video sneak, Madonna × Charli’s first collab drops today, Nathan Fielder’s Elizabeth Holmes trailer is freaking people out, and Brad Bird’s long-gestating Ray Gunn finally has a trailer.
Music

Taylor Swift’s Showgirl: The Encore drops Friday — “Patient Zero” video teases Dakota Johnson & Colin Farrell

Wednesday’s reveal: The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore adds four new tracks — “Patient Zero,” “Cleveland!,” “Pink Clouding,” and “Babylon” — written in Sweden with Max Martin and Shellback after the album’s record first week. It hits DSPs Friday (Sept. 25). This morning Cosmopolitan posted a CBS Mornings sneak of the “Patient Zero” video: a moody drama starring Dakota Johnson and Colin Farrell, with the full clip premiering at Sunday’s VMAs, where Swift also receives MTV’s inaugural Artist Director Honors.

Madonna × Charli XCX “Danceteria Afterhours” is out today

Their first-ever collaboration — a Stuart Price-produced remix of Madonna’s Confessions II cut “Danceteria” — hits streaming today via Warner Records, with limited-edition CD singles also on Madonna’s store. The teaser (“You wanna talk about Madonna and Charli?”) followed their Paris Fashion Week Club Confessions party with Saint Laurent. Madge leads this year’s VMAs with a pile of nominations; Charli keeps the Brat-adjacent club-pop lane wide open.

Film

Nathan Fielder’s Elizabeth Holmes trailer has people calling it a horror movie

A24 dropped the first full trailer for You Can See Everything, the Fielder/Lance Oppenheim doc that began when Holmes invited them into her home 34 days before prison. Shot secretly over three years (Telluride surprise premiere), it follows Holmes and husband Billy Evans through incarceration and after. Social reaction is pure uncanny valley — forced tears, long stares, “documentary shot like a horror film.” Theatrical U.S. open: Oct. 16; NYFF on Sept. 30. Runtime: a bracing 174 minutes.

Movies

Brad Bird’s Ray Gunn trailer: Sam Rockwell, Scarlett Johansson, 70mm then Netflix

First trailer for Bird’s ~30-year passion project: hard-boiled animated sci-fi noir in Metropia, a 1939-flavored future where private eye Raymond Gunn (Rockwell) and one-eyed alien partner Eyera (Tom Waits) dig into murder, aliens, and multimedia star Venus Nova (Johansson). Netflix + Skydance Animation; Michael Giacchino scores. Select U.S. theaters (including limited 70mm) Dec. 4; global Netflix Dec. 18. BFI London Fest premiere first.
