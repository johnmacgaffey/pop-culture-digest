Movies

Ben Affleck’s Animals hits Netflix, and critics are not kind

Affleck directs, co-writes and stars as Mark Kimball, a long-shot Los Angeles mayoral candidate whose son is kidnapped days before the election. Kerry Washington plays his wife Abigail, Gillian Anderson is the ransom specialist who tells them whatever secrets they’ve kept need to come out now, and Steven Yeun is his campaign manager. It’s streaming now on Netflix and playing in select theaters.

Reviews are rough. RogerEbert.com calls it “easily Affleck’s worst directorial effort,” the AP gives it two stars out of four, and Deadline says it’s “stretched too thin.” Several critics point out how closely the setup follows Kurosawa’s High and Low, which Spike Lee just remade as Highest 2 Lowest, without crediting it. Variety’s Guy Lodge is kinder, calling it “nasty” and “compulsive.”

Music

Troye Sivan releases She’s the Best, a looser, late-night follow-up to Something to Give Each Other

The 12-track album is out today on Interscope, with Nicole Kidman starring in the title-track video, a Madonna “Music” interpolation on “Party,” Charli XCX on backing vocals and Lily-Rose Depp on the closer, “Lost Time.” Jack Antonoff and the 1975’s George Daniel are among the collaborators. Sivan has said he wanted it to feel a little “sloppy” compared with his 2023 dance-pop record.

Critics mostly like it: Clash gives it 8/10, NME calls it a “pleasantly confounding” trip through ’90s alt-dance, and it’s averaging 80 on Album of the Year. The video for “FF,” which Sivan calls the gayest of his career, is driving most of the online chatter, along with a debate about whether openly gay pop stars get judged differently.

Music & Awards

Taylor Swift will close the Academy Museum Gala as her Oscar push for “I Knew It, I Knew You” heats up

The Academy Museum announced Thursday that Swift will perform to end its sixth annual gala in Los Angeles on Oct. 17, a night honoring Charlize Theron, Colman Domingo and John Carpenter. The gala is a must-stop for Oscar hopefuls, and Swift is in the original song race for “I Knew It, I Knew You,” her end-credits song for Toy Story 5.

The set list hasn’t been announced, but everyone expects that song to be in it. It became her first No. 1 on U.S. country radio in 13 years and could give her a first Oscar nomination.

TV & Streaming

Crazy Rich Asians is coming back as an HBO series, with Constance Wu, Henry Golding and Michelle Yeoh

HBO has ordered eight episodes that pick up after the 2018 film. Wu and Golding return as Rachel and Nick, Ronny Chieng is back as Eddie, and Michelle Yeoh will recur as Nick’s mother, Eleanor. Film co-writer Adele Lim is showrunner, Jon M. Chu executive produces, and production starts next year.

Fans have waited years for a sequel to the box-office hit, which was stuck in development. It’s also one of the first big HBO orders since the Paramount–Warner Bros. merger closed.
