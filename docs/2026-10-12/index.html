Movies

Other Mommy opens to $27.5M; Sorkin’s Social Reckoning bombs at $5.5M

Universal’s Jessica Chastain horror Other Mommy (Rob Savage, James Wan / Blumhouse–Atomic Monster) took the weekend with an estimated $27.5 million from 3,365 theaters — a solid debut on a roughly $19 million budget, even with an 18% Rotten Tomatoes score and a D+ CinemaScore. Audiences still showed up for Halloween-month mommy horror; demos were nearly even male/female, with 18- to 24-year-olds the biggest cohort.

Aaron Sorkin’s Facebook whistleblower drama The Social Reckoning — Mikey Madison as Frances Haugen, Jeremy Allen White as a Wall Street Journal reporter, Jeremy Strong as Mark Zuckerberg — opened fifth with just $5.5 million from 2,606 theaters, well under forecasts. Holdovers Verity, Resident Evil, and Brad Pitt’s Heart of the Beast all outgrossed it. Domestic year-to-date crossed $8 billion on Friday.

Celebrity

Dan Levy calls out Shane Gillis’s SNL monologue: “More men need to be standing up”

A day after Gillis’s third SNL hosting gig lit up the internet, Schitt’s Creek star Dan Levy posted an Instagram Reel pushing back on the gym-clothes bit in which Gillis joked that women “dress like whores now.” “Women wearing short shorts at the gym does not make them whores. It makes the men calling them whores misogynists,” Levy said, calling himself “speechless that something like that can exist on live national television.”

Levy, who hosted SNL in 2021, argued that “more men need to be standing up in opposition” because complacency helps create an environment where assault is treated as a “conversation” instead of a crime. Elizabeth Banks cheered “Nailed it” in the comments. Reps for SNL and Gillis didn’t immediately comment to Variety. Gillis also slipped an F-bomb during Saturday’s live broadcast.

Music

Taylor Swift hits 100 career weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard 200

The Life of a Showgirl held No. 1 for a second straight week of its Encore rebound — and a 14th week overall — on 93,000 equivalent units. That push gave Swift 100 cumulative weeks atop the Billboard 200 across her catalog, extending her solo-artist record. Elvis Presley is next among soloists with 67; only the Beatles (132) sit higher all-time.

The Encore deluxe (four new tracks, including “Patient Zero”) still counts with the original for chart math. Drake’s reissued Habibti jumped to No. 2, Olivia Rodrigo held No. 3, and the Beatles’ Rubber Soul re-entered at No. 4 — its first top-10 stay since April 1966.

Streaming

Ben Affleck’s Animals is No. 1 on Netflix worldwide — reviews be damned

Affleck’s L.A. crime thriller — which he directed and co-wrote, starring Kerry Washington, Gillian Anderson, and Steven Yeun — is the No. 1 movie on Netflix in all but 14 of 93 countries tracked by FlixPatrol. The plot: a mayoral candidate and his wife scramble after their son is kidnapped, and the secrets between them start to surface.

Critics have not been kind (35% on Rotten Tomatoes; Vulture called it “a catastrophe”), and audiences aren’t much warmer (41% Popcornmeter). That hasn’t slowed the global binge. Polygon notes the same “junky-but-fun” streaming wave that recently lifted Robert De Niro’s The Whisper Man.
