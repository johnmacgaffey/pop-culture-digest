Movies

Greta Gerwig’s first Narnia trailer is here, and nobody agrees on it

Netflix dropped the first trailer for Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew on Tuesday after Gerwig unveiled it at an IMAX event in New York, where Stephen Colbert interviewed her. It’s scored to Led Zeppelin’s famously hard-to-license “Stairway to Heaven” and opens on Daniel Craig’s Uncle Andrew handing two kids a magic ring, then races through the Wood Between the Worlds, Emma Mackey’s Jadis (the future White Witch), a flying horse, and a glimpse of Aslan, voiced by Meryl Streep, singing Narnia into existence.

Reactions range from “best trailer I’ve seen in years” to “a bizarre, disastrous fever dream,” with the CGI-heavy look and Streep’s casting as the lion drawing the most fire. It’s one of Netflix’s biggest swings, with a reported $280 million budget. It opens in theaters and IMAX on Feb. 12, 2027, and streams on Netflix April 2.

TV

Girls is coming back: HBO orders a five-episode limited run

Lena Dunham returns as Hannah, joined by Allison Williams (Marnie), Jemima Kirke (Jessa), Zosia Mamet (Shoshanna) and Andrew Rannells (Elijah). Production starts next year, 15 years after the show premiered and 10 years after the Season 6 finale. Dunham writes, directs and executive produces again alongside Judd Apatow.

HBO’s comedy chief Amy Gravitt framed it as Dunham turning “her gaze from the tumult of one’s 20s to the milestone of turning 40,” adding that “the world has changed since we last saw” the gang. HBO announced it with a short video of the cast.

Celebrity

Kelly Clarkson vs. a Brooklyn shop over a $15,000 mechanical bird, and now it’s “for sale” at $15 million

On Saturday Clarkson told her 8.8 million followers on X that Art of Play, a curiosity shop in Brooklyn Heights, looked at her “as if I couldn’t afford” Molly, a wooden singing automaton on its wall, and compared it to the Pretty Woman boutique scene. The shop says Molly is bolted to the wall and was never for sale, the $15,000 tag was the price of commissioning a custom bird from its Seattle maker, and staff offered her a $2,000 alternative. Clarkson fired back calling the owner “rude” and a “liar.”

Co-owner Adam Rubin told USA Today the shop has been hit with threatening calls and its neighbors doxxed, but sales and foot traffic are surging and locals have left pastries for the staff. His response: he listed Molly at $15 million. Leaked security footage of the visit surfaced Tuesday and keeps the argument going.

Olivia Rodrigo and Chappell Roan team up on an “expectations” remix, out Thursday

Rodrigo confirmed it on Instagram with a photo of the two on a yellow car with the plate “XPECT8NS”: the remix and a video directed by Bardia Zeinali arrive Thursday, Oct. 15, at 5 p.m. PT. It’s Roan’s first-ever feature, produced by their shared collaborator Dan Nigro. Rumors started Sunday when a German retailer briefly listed the single, then Rodrigo teased a clip of a voice saying, “Ooh, Olivia does remixes now.”

“expectations” is track 12 on you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, which opened at No. 1 with 485,000 units, 2026’s biggest solo debut. The remix isn’t streaming yet, so the link below is the original album version.

TV

Ryan Coogler’s X-Files reboot gets a full series order at Hulu

Danielle Deadwyler and Himesh Patel star as “two highly decorated but vastly different FBI agents” assigned to a long-shuttered unit for unexplained cases. They’re new characters, not new versions of Mulder and Scully. Coogler wrote and directed the pilot and executive produces with showrunner Jennifer Yale and original creator Chris Carter.

It’s Coogler’s highest-profile project since Sinners set the all-time record with 16 Oscar nominations and won four, including his screenplay and best actor for Michael B. Jordan. There’s no trailer or premiere date yet.
