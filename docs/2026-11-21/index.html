Pop Culture Digest
What dominated the last two days
The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opened with $21 million in previews and is headed for a $110 million weekend. Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI made $3.79 billion on its first day, Netflix’s FTX drama The Altruists premiered, and BTS opened its Asia tour and won Artist of the Year at MAMA.
Movies

Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is headed for a $110 million opening

Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opened Friday after making $21 million in previews from Wednesday and Thursday shows. That’s the second-best preview in franchise history, behind Catching Fire’s $25.2 million, and the second-best of 2026 after Spider-Man: Brand New Day. By Friday midday, Deadline projected a $51 million first day and a $110 million-plus domestic opening, more than double the $44.6 million that the last prequel, The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, opened to in 2023. Lionsgate had been forecasting about $80 million.

Francis Lawrence directs the adaptation of Suzanne Collins’ prequel novel, set 24 years before the first book. Joseph Zada plays a young Haymitch Abernathy, the role Woody Harrelson played in the original films, with Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin and Maya Hawke. Critics are lukewarm, with about 67% on Rotten Tomatoes, but audiences gave it a 95% score, the best in the series.

Games

GTA VI makes $3.79 billion on day one, the biggest entertainment launch ever

Rockstar said Friday that Grand Theft Auto VI made $3.79 billion in retail sales worldwide on its first day, Thursday. Rockstar says that’s a record for any entertainment launch, in any medium. The game also sold 32.5 million copies before release, another record. CNBC notes that’s nearly five times the roughly $800 million GTA V made on its first day in 2013. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world,” said Rockstar founder Sam Houser.

The music is part of the launch, too. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a 34-track soundtrack from Atlantic and Rockstar, came out Friday with songs from Travis Scott, Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Morgan Wallen, Cardi B, JENNIE, Lana Del Rey and Justice (with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard). Lindsey Buckingham, Keith Richards, Bad Bunny, PinkPantheress and Jason Sudeikis are among the in-game radio DJs.

TV

Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle play the FTX couple in Netflix’s The Altruists

Netflix released The Altruists on Thursday, an eight-episode drama about the rise and fall of the crypto exchange FTX. Anthony Boyle plays Sam Bankman-Fried and Julia Garner plays Caroline Ellison, his on-and-off girlfriend who ran the sister firm Alameda. Graham Moore (The Imitation Game) created it. The show moves between their 2016 meet-cute at a New York trading firm and the 2022 collapse in the Bahamas, centered on the $8 billion hole where customer deposits should have been. Karan Soni, Alex Lawther, Paul Reiser and Matt Rife co-star.

Critics loved the two leads and were split on the rest. The Guardian called Boyle and Garner “two of the best young screen actors working today.” The Hollywood Reporter called the show “all heart and not enough head,” arguing that its focus on the romance shortchanges the fraud and the people it hurt.

Music

BTS opens its Asia tour in Taiwan and wins Artist of the Year at MAMA

BTS opened the Asia-Pacific leg of its Arirang world tour Thursday at Kaohsiung National Stadium in Taiwan, with more shows there Saturday and Sunday. Kaohsiung lit the 85 Sky Tower, Love River and other landmarks in purple, ran a “Bus 613” shuttle (a nod to the band’s June 13 debut) and changed some crosswalk signals to read “Army Stop” and “Army Go.” Saturday’s show will be broadcast in theaters in more than 80 countries and regions. The leg runs 26 shows in nine cities through Melbourne and Sydney in February.

On Friday, BTS won Artist of the Year at the MAMA Awards in Osaka for the eighth time, plus best male group. Times Now counts that as the group’s 80th daesang, or grand prize. ENHYPEN won the most awards on night one. Days earlier, BTS won six awards at the Melon Music Awards, including album of the year for Arirang.
