What dominated the last two days

Movies

Sunrise on the Reaping opens to $120 million and revives The Hunger Games

Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping made an estimated $120 million in North America from 4,018 theaters, plus $105 million from 84 international markets, for a $225 million global opening. Lionsgate had projected just over $80 million. The domestic total includes $21 million in Thursday previews, a $56 million Friday, $36.3 million Saturday and $27.7 million Sunday. It’s the first $100 million opening since Spider-Man: Brand New Day in July and the fifth-biggest of 2026.

That’s nearly triple the $44.6 million debut of the last prequel, The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, and roughly level with Mockingjay – Part 1’s $121.8 million, making it the franchise’s fourth-best opening. Audiences gave it an A- CinemaScore, and 67% of ticket buyers were women. Domestic IMAX added $16.3 million, the franchise’s biggest IMAX opening. Peter Farrelly’s Stallone biopic I Play Rocky had a weak wide expansion the same weekend.

Celebrity

Miley Cyrus marries Maxx Morando at Frank Sinatra’s former Palm Springs estate

Miley Cyrus, 33, married Liily drummer Maxx Morando, 28, on Saturday, Nov. 21, in a poolside ceremony in Palm Springs, at an estate once owned by Frank Sinatra. The Flaming Lips’ Wayne Coyne officiated. About 100 guests came, reportedly under an all-black dress code, including Tish Cyrus, Noah Cyrus, and Billy Ray Cyrus with Elizabeth Hurley. Cyrus wore a long-sleeved white gown by Anthony Vaccarello for Saint Laurent, and Morando wore a black Saint Laurent suit.

Cyrus shared black-and-white photos on Instagram captioned “M+M” and the date. The wedding came a month after the couple sang “Let’s Get Married,” from her new album Bass Persuades, together at the Hollywood Bowl. The date and location had been kept secret.

Music

ATEEZ score a fourth No. 1 album as U2 returns to the top 10

ATEEZ’s GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6 debuts at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 dated Nov. 28 with 195,000 equivalent units, 191,000 of them pure sales, helped by 32 CD and vinyl variants. It’s the group’s fourth No. 1 and 10th straight top 10, which passes Stray Kids for the most top 10s by any group this decade. Olivia Rodrigo’s you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love drops to No. 2, and Morgan Wallen’s I’m the Problem holds at No. 3.

U2’s new album Carnaval de Luz debuts at No. 4 with 49,000 units. It’s the band’s 14th top 10 and its first since 2023’s Songs of Surrender.

Celebrity

Britney Spears apologizes to her family, then deletes the post

On Saturday, Nov. 21, Britney Spears posted a long Instagram apology to her “beautiful family” for “bringing up past issues.” She said she misses them “like crazy” and called her stubbornness “childish and embarrassing.” She also said she had never talked about those issues with them face to face. The post was set over an AI-generated image of two cats having tea in London, and she deleted it soon after.

It’s the warmest public message she’s sent her estranged relatives since her conservatorship ended in 2021 and years of public feuding followed, and entertainment outlets picked it up widely before she took it down.
